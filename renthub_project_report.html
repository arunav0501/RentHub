Executive Abstract

RentHub is an enterprise-grade, peer-to-peer equipment rental ecosystem engineered to resolve structural inefficiencies in modern collaborative consumption. By synthesizing high-performance web architecture (React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Express.js, and Supabase PostgreSQL) with state-of-the-art multimodal artificial intelligence (Google Gemini 3.6 Vision and Flash NLU), RentHub eliminates traditional market frictions. The platform introduces three core engineering innovations: (1) a transparent, server-evaluated 0–100 Trust Score Engine with neutral baseline calibration; (2) an automated AI Image-to-Listing Pipeline with strict anti-hallucination guardrails; and (3) a Smart Rental Trip Planner that translates everyday natural language prompts into live, ranked gear checklists matched against real database inventory. Accompanied by an atomic digital wallet, verified review gating, and dual-role dashboards, RentHub provides a turnkey circular economy marketplace.

RentHub Project Report Chapter 1: Executive Summary

1. Executive Summary & Project Overview

1.1 Project Purpose & Scope

In contemporary society, consumer ownership of specialized equipment is characterized by severe underutilization. Premium electronic hardware, outdoor survival equipment, cinema cameras, musical instruments, and heavy-duty power tools are typically purchased at high capital cost, utilized for brief intervals (e.g., weekend excursions, occasional home renovations, annual musical performances), and subsequently relegated to storage. Conversely, individuals who require temporary access to specialized equipment confront prohibitive purchase costs, opaque rental agencies, and fragmented peer arrangements.

RentHub was conceived and developed as a semester-long Software Engineering project to bridge this divide. It establishes a resilient, decentralized peer-to-peer rental marketplace that empowers individuals to monetize idle physical assets while offering renters economical, frictionless access to top-tier equipment within their community.

1.2 Core Architectural Achievements

RentHub moves beyond typical CRUD applications through five pioneering technical innovations:

  • Dynamic Trust Score Reputation Engine: A real-time, server-side algorithm evaluating transaction fidelity, profile verification, listing comprehensiveness, and cancellation frequency. It mitigates the cold-start barrier with a calibrated neutral baseline (~65/100) for new community members.
  • Multimodal AI Image-to-Listing Generator: Leveraging Google Gemini Vision, the system processes raw photographs of physical items to generate structured catalog drafts (title, category, brand, model, condition, specifications) with mathematically enforced anti-hallucination guardrails.
  • Smart Rental Trip Planner: A conversational natural-language discovery pipeline combining Gemini Flash NLU with real-time relational inventory querying. The planner extracts travel intent, structures dynamic packing requirements, and executes multi-factor relevance ranking across active database stock.
  • Full-Stack Financial Ledger & Multi-Rail Payments: Integrated digital wallet managing stored-value transactions with ACID-compliant double-entry ledger bookkeeping, overdraft prevention, and multi-rail checkout support (Wallet Balance, UPI, Credit/Debit Card, and Cash on Delivery).
  • Verified Renter Review Moderation: Cryptographically tied review authentication guaranteeing that only renters with finalized, verified bookings can review equipment, maintaining market integrity.

Economic studies in consumer resource sharing reveal that the average power drill is utilized for less than 15 minutes across its entire lifecycle. Similarly, high-end DSLR cameras, audio equipment, and camping gear sit idle for 95% of their operational lifespan. Despite the rapid growth of the collaborative sharing economy (exemplified by Airbnb for lodging and Turo for automobiles), the broader physical goods rental sector remains severely fragmented, plagued by four systemic barriers:

Market Friction Traditional Marketplace Defect RentHub Technical Solution
Trust Deficit & Counterparty Risk Peer platforms rely on easily fabricated self-ratings or subjective text reviews with no verified transaction anchoring. Server-evaluated Trust Score Engine (0–100) computed directly from immutable database actions with tier classification.

2.2 Targeted User Personas

RentHub is engineered to serve three distinct personas across the peer-to-peer ecosystem:

  • The Weekend Adventurer (Renter): A traveler or hobbyist seeking professional camping gear or an action camera for a specific 4-day excursion. They require rapid item discovery, transparent pricing, verified reliability, and flexible checkout.
  • The Content Creator & Freelancer (Renter/Owner): An independent videographer or musician who owns professional gear (e.g., Sony Alpha camera or digital piano). They monetize high-value equipment during idle days while renting supplemental lenses or lighting when large gigs arise.
  • The Practical Homeowner (Owner): An individual with power tools, pressure washers, or party audio equipment interested in safe, low-friction secondary income with verified counterparties and zero cancellation risk.
Unified Role Architecture: In RentHub, every user account is inherently dual-role. Unlike legacy systems that force users to register separate "Lender" and "Borrower" profiles, any authenticated member can seamlessly list products for rent and request bookings for community equipment from a single unified account.

RentHub Project Report Chapter 6: Dynamic Trust Score Engine

6. Algorithmic Deep-Dive: RentHub Trust Score Engine

6.1 Algorithmic Philosophy: Tamper-Proof Reputation

Reputation systems in peer-to-peer markets are frequently vulnerable to manipulation through fake reviews, review bombing, or collusive ratings. RentHub eliminates these failure modes by evaluating reputation exclusively server-side using verifiable relational database transactions. The Trust Score is normalized on a deterministic 0 to 100 integer scale.

6.2 Owner Trust Score Mathematical Model

Owner Trust Score = Profile Score (20%) + Listing Quality (25%) + Rental Fulfillment (35%) + Reliability (20%)
Category & Weight Evaluation Metrics Scoring Logic & Point Allocation
Profile Completeness
(20 pts / 20%)		 Name, Email, Phone verification, and Account Tenure. • Name verified: +5 pts • Email verified: +5 pts
• Phone linked: +5 pts • Tenure: ≥30d (+5), ≥7d (+3), <7d (+1)
Listing Quality
(25 pts / 25%)		 Catalog volume, verified imagery, descriptive depth, specifications. • Catalog Size: min(10, ProductCount * 2.5) pts
• Photo Coverage: (WithImage / TotalProducts) * 5 pts
• Detailed Descriptions (≥40 chars): (WithDesc / Total) * 5 pts
• Specs (Brand/Model/Condition): (WithSpecs / Total) * 5 pts
Rental History & Fulfillment
(35 pts / 35%)		 Volume of completed rentals and approval rate of inbound requests. • Completed Volume: min(20, CompletedRentals * 4) pts
• Fulfillment Rate: ((Completed + Approved) / TotalBookings) * 15 pts
*Brand-new owners receive a neutral baseline of 20 pts.
Reliability & Cancellations
(20 pts / 20%)		 Absence of owner-initiated rental cancellations. • 0 Cancellations: 20 pts (100% Reliability)
• Cancel rate ≤ 5%: 16 pts; ≤ 15%: 10 pts; ≤ 30%: 5 pts; >30%: 0 pts

6.3 Reputation Tiers & The Neutral Baseline Principle

A critical design innovation is the Neutral Baseline Principle. New users are not penalized with an artificial score of 0, nor are they gifted an unearned score of 100. Instead, new community members receive a calibrated baseline of ~65/100 and an explicit "New Member" badge, ensuring immediate participation while incentivizing completed transactions.

RentHub Project Report Chapter 7: Multimodal AI Image-to-Listing

7. Multimodal AI Image-to-Listing Generation

7.1 Architectural Workflow: From Raw Photograph to Structured Catalog

Listing items on traditional platforms is tedious, causing high owner drop-off rates. RentHub incorporates Google Gemini 3.6 Flash Vision directly into the item creation flow. An owner uploads a single photo of their equipment; the backend streams the image to Gemini's multimodal encoder, which parses physical features, text labels, and condition cues to generate a structured JSON listing draft in real time.

AI Vision Processing Pipeline
[Owner Browser] ===> (1) POST /api/ai-listing/analyze-image (Multipart/Form-Data)
     |
[Express Backend + Multer] ===> (2) Validates MIME & reads binary buffer
     |
[Gemini Vision Model Client] ===> (3) Base64 image payload + System Prompt + JSON Schema
     |
[Gemini 3.6 Flash Inference] ===> (4) Decodes Title, Description, Category, Brand, Model, Condition
     |
[Backend Validation] ===> (5) Maps category to existing DB Category ID
     |
[Frontend Add Product Modal] ===> (6) Pre-fills form fields; Owner reviews and enters Daily Rent

7.2 Anti-Hallucination Guardrails & Prompt Engineering

Large Vision Models are susceptible to fabricating technical specifications when visual cues are ambiguous. RentHub deploys strict system instructions to guarantee catalog veracity:

  • No Spec Hallucination: Gemini is strictly instructed: "If brand or model is not clearly visible or known, return an empty string ('') rather than guessing."
  • Taxonomy Enforcement: The prompt dynamically injects the exact list of categories currently stored in the database. Gemini must select exclusively from this array or return an empty string.
  • Separation of Pricing & Logistics: Gemini is barred from fabricating rental prices, geographic locations, or item quantities. These parameters remain strictly under the owner's manual control.
  • Conservative Condition Grading: Items cannot be labeled "New" unless unopened packaging or tags are visually apparent; otherwise, they default to "Like New", "Good", or "Fair".

8. Smart Rental Planner & Hybrid Search Engine

8.1 Conversational Gear Discovery Architecture

A traveler planning a trip rarely knows the precise equipment model they need. For example, a user planning a scuba diving and beach trip to Goa requires an underwater camera, waterproof speaker, and high-capacity power bank. Conventional keyword search forces the user to execute multiple disjointed queries.

RentHub's Smart Rental Planner resolves this through a hybrid pipeline combining Gemini Flash NLU with Prisma Relational Inventory Querying.

Smart Rental Planner Hybrid Search Flow
User Input: "I'm going to Goa for 5 days with 4 friends"
     |
     v
[Gemini Flash NLU Engine] ===> Extracts Destination: "Goa", Duration: 5d, Group: 5, Checklist
     |
     v
[Express Database Search] ===> Queries real active inventory in PostgreSQL overlapping dates
     |
     v
[Relevance Scoring Engine (0 - 100)] ===> Title (40) + Brand (20) + Category (20) + Desc (10) + Proximity (10)
     |
     v

8.2 Relevance Scoring Formulation

Relevance Score = Title Match (40) + Brand/Model (20) + Category (20) + Description (10) + Location Proximity (10)
  • Title Match (up to 40 pts): +40 points for exact requirement token matches; +35 points for synonym keywords.
  • Brand/Model Match (up to 20 pts): +20 points when identified brand or model strings align with product metadata.
  • Category Match (20 pts): +20 points when product belongs to the suggested marketplace category.
  • Description Match (10 pts): +10 points for contextual keyword matches within the product description body.
  • Location Proximity (10 pts): +10 bonus points when the equipment's physical location matches the trip destination.
  • False Positive Clamping: Items scoring below 45 points are pruned to eliminate irrelevant search results.

8.3 Live Price Estimation & Dynamic Duration Calculations

RentHub Project Report Chapter 9: Financial Architecture & Digital Wallet

9. Financial Architecture: Digital Wallet & Payment Gateway

9.1 Stored-Value Digital Wallet & Double-Entry Ledger

To facilitate seamless peer transactions, RentHub features an integrated Digital Wallet. The wallet eliminates the friction of third-party payment redirects for repeated rentals, providing instant top-up, zero-latency booking checkout, automated cancellation refunds, and owner payout disbursements.

Transaction Type Direction Ledger Accounting & Reconciliation Logic
TOPUP CREDIT (+) User funds wallet via UPI or Card. Wallet balance increments atomically inside a database transaction; creates an immutable WalletTransaction record.
RENTAL_PAYMENT DEBIT (-) Deducted instantly upon booking confirmation. Booking status set to PAID with generated payment reference ID (TXN-RH-*).
RENTAL_EARNING CREDIT (+) Credited to the product owner's wallet once the rental is marked COMPLETED.
REFUND CREDIT (+) Triggered automatically if an owner rejects a pending booking or a rental is cancelled within allowed policy windows.
WITHDRAWAL DEBIT (-) Owner or renter disburses funds to their linked bank account or UPI VPA. Subject to strict overdraft verification.

9.2 Overdraft Protection & Atomic Concurrency

All wallet adjustments execute within atomic database transactions via prisma.$transaction. If a user attempts to withdraw or spend more than their confirmed balance, the transaction aborts with an HTTP 400 Bad Request error: 

// Atomic Withdrawal with Overdraft Protection (wallet.controller.js)
const result = await prisma.$transaction(async (tx) => {
  const user = await tx.user.findUnique({ where: { id: userId } });
  if (user.walletBalance < amount) {
    const error = new Error('Insufficient wallet balance');
    error.status = 400;
    throw error;
  }
  const updatedUser = await tx.user.update({
    where: { id: userId },
    data: { walletBalance: { decrement: amount } },
  });
  return await tx.walletTransaction.create({
    data: {
      userId,
      amount,
      type: 'DEBIT',
      category: 'WITHDRAWAL',
      referenceId: `TXN-RH-WDL-${Date.now()}`,
    },
  });
});
RentHub Project Report Chapter 10: Reviews, Dashboard & UI/UX

10. Verified Reviews, Dual-Role Dashboard & UI/UX

10.1 Verified Renter Review Moderation Engine

Customer reviews in traditional marketplaces are frequently tainted by competitor sabotage or fraudulent ratings. RentHub enforces Cryptographic Review Verification:

  • Booking Verification Gate: A user can only submit a review if they possess an active or finalized booking (status: COMPLETED or APPROVED) for that specific item in the database.
  • Self-Review Prevention: Product owners are categorically prevented from reviewing their own listings.
  • One Review Per Rental: A unique database constraint on bookingId ensures that each rental cycle permits exactly one review, preventing review duplication.
  • Dynamic Aggregate Rating Breakdown: The system automatically calculates aggregate average ratings (e.g., 4.9★) and dynamic percentage distributions across 1-star to 5-star brackets.

10.2 Booking State Machine & Dual-Role Dashboard

Rental Lifecycle Finite State Machine
  [Renter Request] ===> [PENDING] ---> [APPROVED] ---> [COMPLETED] (Unlocks Review)
                              \            |
                               \---> [REJECTED] / [CANCELLED]

The unified Dashboard provides tabbed views for both roles:

  • My Bookings (Renter View): Tracks active rentals, booking payment statuses, start/end dates, total pricing, and cancellation triggers.
  • My Inventory (Owner View): Displays all active listings with photo previews, daily rates, quantities, and quick deletion/edit actions.
  • Rental Requests (Owner View): Incoming rental applications requiring owner action with one-click Approve, Reject, and Mark as Completed controls.

10.3 Global Dark Mode & Adaptive Design System

RentHub Project Report Chapter 12: Security Architecture

12. Security Architecture & Data Protection

12.1 Defense-in-Depth Security Strategy

RentHub implements multi-layered security controls to protect user personal data, equipment listings, and financial transactions against common cyber vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10):

Security Layer Threat Mitigation Technical Implementation
Stateless Authentication Session hijacking, credential theft. Stateless JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with a high-entropy secret; short-lived tokens transmitted via Bearer authorization headers.
Cryptographic Storage Database credential exposure. Passwords hashed using bcryptjs with 10 salt rounds; plaintext passwords never touch database logs or responses.
Input Validation Injection attacks, payload tampering, malformed requests. Strict schema validation using Zod on every inbound request body; rejects unverified fields before hitting controller logic.
SQL Injection Immunity Arbitrary database query execution. 100% parameterization through Prisma ORM; raw string concatenation is strictly avoided across all repository queries.
HTTP Security Headers Clickjacking, XSS, MIME sniffing. Helmet.js automatically injects protective headers (Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options).
CORS Enforcement Cross-origin unauthorized API calls. Restricted Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) whitelisting authorized frontend domains and standard REST headers.
Financial Integrity Double spending, negative balances, race conditions. ACID-compliant transactions (prisma.$transaction) with balance checks; immutable transaction ledger.
Key Isolation Principle: External AI API keys (Google Gemini) and database connection URIs are strictly encapsulated within backend environment variables (server/.env) and are never exposed to the client bundle.

13.2 Critical Edge Cases Verified

  • Wallet Overdraft Prevention: Attempting to withdraw ₹999,999 with a ₹3,250 balance correctly triggers an HTTP 400 Bad Request response with "Insufficient wallet balance" without corrupting user state.
  • Unverified Review Rejection: A user attempting to review a product without a finalized or active booking is rejected with: "Only verified renters with a completed or active booking can review this item."
  • Ambiguous Trip Intent Clarification: Submitting a sparse prompt (e.g. "I'm going trekking") activates the clarification handler: returns isClarificationNeeded: true with a prompt asking for destination, duration, or group size.
  • AI Vision API Outage Resilience: If the Gemini API key is missing or network connectivity is severed, the system returns an informative HTTP 503 status code while leaving the manual product creation workflow fully operational.
  • Booking Date Conflict Prevention: Overlapping date requests for products with 0 remaining inventory units are flagged as unavailable, preventing double bookings.

15. Strategic Product Roadmap, Engineering Reflections & Conclusion

15.1 Strategic Product Roadmap

While the current v2.0 release delivers a production-ready, feature-complete marketplace, several high-impact engineering enhancements are planned for future versions:

  • Interactive Geospatial Mapping (v2.1): Integration of Leaflet and OpenStreetMap to provide map-based radius searching, allowing renters to discover equipment within walking distance.
  • Real-Time P2P Chat via WebSockets (v2.2): A bidirectional in-app messaging engine (Socket.io) enabling owners and renters to coordinate item handovers directly without sharing personal phone numbers.
  • Automated Escrow Security Deposits (v2.3): Temporary pre-authorization holds on renter wallets or cards during active rentals, released automatically upon safe return.
  • AI Dynamic Pricing Engine (v2.4): Machine learning models analyzing regional seasonal demand (e.g. Goa in December vs. July) to recommend optimal daily rental rates to owners.

15.2 Key Engineering Reflections

The design and implementation of RentHub provided valuable real-world software engineering insights:

  1. AI Guardrails are Essential for System Veracity: Incorporating generative AI into a transactional system requires strict prompt constraints, JSON schema validation, and database anchoring. Separating language comprehension from inventory truth prevented hallucinations and preserved system integrity.
  2. Reputation Systems Require Objective Data: Subjective reviews are inadequate in high-value asset sharing. Deriving trust scores from verifiable database actions (profile completion, cancellations, fulfillment rates) established a transparent, tamper-proof foundation.
  3. Atomic Financial Transactions Guarantee Reliability: Implementing double-entry transaction logging and overdraft prevention through database transactions ensured financial consistency even during simulated concurrent access.

15.3 Project Conclusion

RentHub successfully demonstrates the practical synergy between modern full-stack web engineering and state-of-the-art multimodal artificial intelligence. By unifying a responsive React 19 frontend, an Express.js API gateway, a Supabase PostgreSQL relational database, and Google Gemini Vision and NLU models, RentHub delivers a robust, secure, and user-centric peer-to-peer equipment rental ecosystem.

Through its transparent Trust Score engine, 1-click AI listing creation, natural language travel planner, integrated digital wallet, and verified review moderation, RentHub provides a compelling blueprint for the future of the collaborative sharing economy.
